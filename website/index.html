Busy Peasy

All your Google and Microsoft 365 calendars in one app, with reminders you can't miss.

Download for Windows

    Free. Your calendars stay on your computer.

    IFeatures

    Every calendar, one place.

    • Google and Microsoft 365 accounts side by side, as many as you need.
    • Day, Week, Month and Schedule views, and search.
    • Reminders you can't miss: a window comes up on top of your apps, with a Join button for calls.
    • Add, move and change events, invite people, and answer invites.
    • Your AI agents, like Claude Code and Codex, can read and plan your calendar, only if you allow them.
    • Lives in the tray and starts with Windows.

    IIPrivacy

    Your calendars stay yours.

    • Your calendars are saved on your computer. There is no account with us.
    • Busy Peasy talks only to Google and Microsoft, to sync the calendars you connect, and to GitHub, to check for updates.
    • Busy Peasy asks before a new agent can use your calendar. You can turn agent access off at any time.
    • No tracking, no ads, and no cookies on this site.
